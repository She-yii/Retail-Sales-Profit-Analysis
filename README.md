# Retail-Sales-and-Profit-Dashboard

## Overview

This project is an interactive **Retail Sales & Profit Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Excel**.
It gives a clear breakdown of retail performance from 2022 to 2023 showing which categories did best, how profit and sales moved, and how customer age affected purchases.
I created it mainly to visualize insights that would help any retail business understand where their money is coming from and how their customers purchase.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Objectives

Here's what I wanted to find out from the data:
1. Which product category brings in the most profit?
2. Do high sales automatically mean high profit margins?
3. Does age have any influence on what customers buy?
4. Which months recorded peak sales?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Insights of Dashboard

### 1️⃣ Which Category Generates the Highest Profit?

-   **Electronics** came out top with around **₦788K** in profit.
-   **Clothing** was close with **₦785K**.
-   **Beauty** recorded the lowest at **₦704K**.

This shows that all three are doing relatively well with Electronics taking the lead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2️⃣ Do High Sales Categories Deliver Strong Margin?

-   **Clothing** had the **strongest profit margin (36%)**, even though it didn't have the highest volume.
-   **Electronics** led in total sales but had a slightly lower margin (33%).
-   **Beauty** had both lower sales and lower margin (31%).

This reveals that more sales don't always equal more profit, it depends on how efficiently the category performs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 3️⃣ Does Age Influence the Type of Category Purchased?

-   Younger customers (**18--35**) are more likely to buy **Beauty and Clothing**.
-   The **36--55** group spends more across **all categories**, especially on **Electronics**.
-   The **56--65** group bought things in moderation with **Electronics** leading.

Beauty and fashion appeal more to younger customers, while older customers go for durable products like Electronics.

### 4️⃣ Sales Trends Over Time

The line chart shows how sales moved from month to month.
- Sales peaked **between September and December**, which makes sense for festive and holiday shopping seasons.
- There was a noticeable drop around **January--March**.
- Comparing both years, **2023 did better overall** because there was a more stable and upward flow of sales.

Sales are seasonal, businesses can plan their stock better in these periods.

## Tools & Techniques

-   **Tool Used:** Microsoft Excel
-   **Main Features:** Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Dashboard Layouts
-   **Steps I Took:**
    -   Cleaned and organized raw data
    -   Built pivot tables for analysis
    -   Created different charts to visualize the story
    -   Added slicers (for category, gender, and year) to make the dashboard interactive

## Key Takeaways

-   **Electronics** made the most profit.
-   **Clothing** had the **best margin performance**.
-   **Younger customers** go for Beauty and Clothing more.
-   **Sales peak during festive months (Sept--Dec)**.
-   **Growth trend** seen from 2022 to 2023.
